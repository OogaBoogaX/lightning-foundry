# 0007. Adopt The Ooga Booga License

**Status:** accepted, 2026-09-30. Supersedes [0004](0004-provisional-license.md) and
[0006](0006-open-contributions.md).

## Decision

Use the same Ooga Booga License text as the other OogaBoogaX repositories for the current
Foundry tree and future contributions. Update the contribution policy and pull request template
to match.

## Alternatives

- Keep Apache-2.0 as the repository license.
- Continue asking contributors to offer both Apache-2.0 and the Unlicense.

## Why

The organization is adopting one public-domain dedication across its projects. Contributions
made during the provisional period were already offered under the Unlicense as well as Apache-2.0,
which allows their use in the current tree under the new dedication.

## Consequences

- Historical Apache-2.0 and Unlicense grants are not revoked; earlier releases remain available
  under their original terms.
- New contributions are offered under The Ooga Booga License.
- The license for any future model weights still needs its own decision.
